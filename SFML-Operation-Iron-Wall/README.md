# Operation Iron Wall

A retro-style 2D military defense game built in **C++** using **SFML**.

## Overview

Operation Iron Wall is a side-scrolling defense game where the player must protect a military tank from waves of enemy soldiers and drones. Soldiers advance on foot while drones attack from the air with rockets, forcing the player to defend both themselves and the tank.

This project was developed to strengthen my understanding of C++, Object-Oriented Programming, and real-time game development.

---

## Gameplay

- Defend the tank from enemy soldiers and drones.
- Shoot enemies before they reach the player or the tank.
- Soldiers deal continuous damage on contact.
- Drones fire rockets from a distance.
- Survive as long as possible while increasing your kill count.

---

## Features

- About, Menu, Pause and Game Over screens
- Sprite sheet animations
- Enemy AI
- Drone projectile system
- Collision detection
- Player and tank health system
- Kill counters
- Blood effects
- HUD
- Pixel-art graphics
- Delta-time based movement
- Modular animation and text classes
